### Changed

- **Each town service wears its own icon.** Trade supplies, bags, books and weapons and armour vendors show the icon of what they sell, weapon masters show crossed swords and portal trainers a portal, so no map pin borrows another service's icon (#978).
- **Less clutter on city maps.** Vendor pins start hidden, like repairs; turn any of them on under *Town services* in the map's filter menu (#978).
